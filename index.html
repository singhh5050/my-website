How Do World Action Models Use Their Imagined Futures? Evidence from Mechanistic Interventions
Nicole H. Ma*, Harsh Singh*
* Equal contribution
NeurIPS Robot Learning Workshop, 2026 (Oral Presentation)

We use activation patching to reveal how robot policies use imagined futures for manipulation. By intervening on these future representations, we redirect robot actions and identify an attention pathway carrying their causal influence.

Efficient Long-Context Continued Pretraining with Delta Attention
Harsh Singh, Jeffrey Willette, Krishna C Puvvada
Work done at NVIDIA.
NeurIPS Pre-to-Post & LCFM Workshops, 2026

We introduce a sparse attention method based on Delta Attention that controls how gradients flow through reused corrections, cutting update time by 23% while retaining long-context comprehension and near-ceiling retrieval accuracy.
